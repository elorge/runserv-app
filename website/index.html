How a company pays Runserv

No manual chasing. The company’s reference does the matching.

See where to pay

The company’s Billing page lists Runserv’s bank accounts and a reference for its wallet and for each open invoice.

Transfer from your bank

Pay into any listed account and put the reference in the narration. Use whichever bank suits you.

It settles itself

When the bank reports the credit, it is matched to the right wallet or invoice and shows on the company’s dashboard and in Runserv’s records.

Anything unclear (a missing reference, a wrong amount) goes to a review queue instead of being guessed, and manual entries need a second person’s approval.
