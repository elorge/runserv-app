Questions

Short answers. Ask us anything else below.

Where does Runserv operate?

We are starting in Lagos. Tell us your city in the form and we will say when we get there.

Do drivers need a wallet balance to take company trips?

No. Drivers accept company trips without any wallet balance, and no cash is collected.

Can a company pay on credit?

Every company starts prepaid. Monthly credit is switched on only after we verify the company and agree a limit and written terms.

How do fleets get paid?

Settlements show each trip, our fee and your net. Only owners, admins and finance staff can see the money.

Is my data safe?

We collect only what is needed to run the service. See the privacy notice.
